## Why it matters

UVM can't pay you until your I-9 is on record. Doing it early avoids delays and keeps UVM in line with federal rules.

## Steps

1. Email UVM Human Resource Services at hrinfo@uvm.edu to check whether you already have an I-9 on record.
2. If you don't, go to the HR office in room 228 of the Waterman Building to fill one out. It is quick.
3. Bring an [acceptable form of ID](https://www.uscis.gov/i-9-central/form-i-9-acceptable-documents).

## How to finish

Comment "ADM-02 done" on your tracking issue and tick the box. **Never post your ID, documents or any personal details in a comment.**
