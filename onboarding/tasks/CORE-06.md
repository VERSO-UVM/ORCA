## Why it matters

Transform to Open Science (TOPS) was part of NASA's [Open-Source Science Initiative](https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/open-science/), a long-term commitment to a scientific culture ready for 21st-century challenges. ORCA used its Open Science 101 course for onboarding until it was taken down in 2025.

This is where open source matters. The course was released under an open Creative Commons license, and we had forked it. So we gave it new life: we took the original materials, extended them, and repurposed them for ORCA.
