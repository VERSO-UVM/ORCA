## Why it matters

As a verified student, GitHub gives you more for free: free use of GitHub Codespaces (up to 180 core hours a month on your personal account), GitHub Copilot, and offers on industry tools in the Student Developer Pack. See [GitHub Education for students](https://docs.github.com/en/education/about-github-education/github-education-for-students/about-github-education-for-students).

Approval isn't instant, so apply in your first week. If you are already verified, skip this task.

**About Copilot:** GitHub Copilot is not on UVM's [approved AI tools](https://www.uvm.edu/ai/supported-and-approved-ai-tools) list. Use it only with public code and public data (see CORE-03).

## Steps

1. Add your UVM email address to your GitHub account and verify it (**Settings > Emails**).
2. Have proof that you are enrolled ready, such as a photo of your UVM ID with a current date, your class schedule, or an enrollment verification letter.
3. Go to your [Education benefits settings](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits) and select **Start an application**.
4. Complete the form and submit it.
5. Once you are approved, open the [GitHub Education portal](https://education.github.com/) to claim your benefits.

The full steps are in GitHub's guide, [Apply to GitHub Education as a student](https://docs.github.com/en/education/about-github-education/github-education-for-students/apply-to-github-education-as-a-student).

## How to finish

Once you are approved, comment "GIT-03 done" on your tracking issue and tick the box. **Your proof of enrollment goes only to GitHub. Never post it in a comment.**
