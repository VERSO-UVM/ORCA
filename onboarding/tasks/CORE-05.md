## Why it matters

An ORCiD (Open Researcher and Contributor ID) tracks your contributions to academic work. It connects your work without depending on your name, which can change over time. We use ORCiDs to credit your work where it fits.

## Steps

1. Go to [orcid.org/register](https://orcid.org/register).
2. Register with your UVM email address and create a password.
3. Enter your name, country and affiliation.
4. Set your security preferences, including a recovery email.
5. Agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Verify your email address with the link ORCID sends you.

## How to finish

Post your reflection on your tracking issue (see [How to finish a task](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task)) and include your ORCiD. Then tick the box.
