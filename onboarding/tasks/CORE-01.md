## Why it matters

As in any job, you need to know the policies and expectations of your role. The wiki holds the details, and we expect you to follow them. Ask if anything is unclear.

Returning students: read both again, since they change between semesters.

## Steps

1. Read the [ORCA wiki](https://github.com/VERSO-UVM/ORCA/wiki).
2. Read the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/VERSO-UVM/ORCA/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How to finish

Send your reflection with the [reflection form](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task), and add your initials and the date to affirm that you have reviewed both.
