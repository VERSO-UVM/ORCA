## Why it matters

ORCA's projects, issues and onboarding all live on GitHub, in the VERSO-UVM organization. You need to be a member to work on them.

## Steps

1. If you don't have one yet, create a GitHub account with a unique username.
2. Send your username to the VERSO Director, who will invite you to the VERSO-UVM organization.
3. Accept the invitation on GitHub's **Your organizations** page.

## How to finish

Comment "ADM-01 done" on your tracking issue and tick the box. Admin tasks need no reflection.
