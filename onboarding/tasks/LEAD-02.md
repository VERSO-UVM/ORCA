## Why it matters

ORCA Pods work in sprints. Running the first few planning meetings and reviews early, even with little to show, gets your team used to the rhythm.

## Steps

**Week 2 (sprint 1)**

1. Hold your first sprint planning meeting and sprint review. There won't be much to show at the review, but it helps the team get a feel for the process.
2. Hold your first coworking time to bring the team together in person. If you'd like to order food, tell the VERSO Director, who can help arrange it.
3. Check the repository's **Insights** to make sure everyone has committed a change and knows how to add to the project.

**Week 3 (sprint 2)**

4. Hold your second sprint planning meeting.

**Week 4 (sprint 2)**

5. Hold your second demo review.

## How to finish

Post your reflection on your tracking issue (see [How to finish a task](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task)) after the week 4 demo review. Then tick the box.
