## Why it matters

Leading a Pod is about trust, safety and how teams work, as much as code. These readings and talks are the shared grounding for every ORCA lead. Returning students do this only the first time they lead.

## Steps

Each week, read or listen to that week's pieces.

**Week 1**

- [How the agile methodology really works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1iccpf2eN1Q), InfoWorld (4 min 30 s)
- [Why good leaders make you feel safe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lmyZMtPVodo), Simon Sinek, TED (11 min 15 s). A great leader makes their team feel secure and draws them into a circle of trust. Creating that trust and safety means taking on big responsibility.

**Week 2**

- [The power of vulnerability](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iCvmsMzlF7o), Brené Brown, TED (20 min 49 s). Brown studies human connection: our ability to empathize, belong and love.
- [Most leaders don't even know the game they're in](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyTQ5-SQYTo), Simon Sinek (35 min 8 s). Trust and cooperation aren't standard in organizations, though we know they should be. Two attributes help leaders build them: empathy and perspective.

**Week 3**

- [NUMMI](https://www.thisamericanlife.org/561/nummi-2015), This American Life (55 min 30 s). In 1984 General Motors and Toyota opened a joint factory, and Toyota showed GM the secrets of its production system. Why couldn't GM learn from it?

**Week 4**

- [Brené Brown on what vulnerability isn't](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2WSsAxkAb3DCjqVmQFIeH1), WorkLife with Adam Grant (41 min 9 s). Showing vulnerability at work, how much is too much, and where to set boundaries.
- [Supercharge your one-on-one meetings](https://hbr.org/podcast/2024/01/supercharge-your-one-on-one-meetings), HBR IdeaCast (32 min 49 s). How to prepare for, structure and follow up on one-on-ones.

## How to finish

By 3pm each Friday, send a paragraph about that week's pieces with the [reflection form](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task): what you found insightful, and what you disagreed with. Choose LEAD-01 and the week.
