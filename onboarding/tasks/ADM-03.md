## Why it matters

PeopleSoft is where you get paid and where you enter your hours.

## Steps

1. Go to myUVM and select the **PeopleSoft** icon in the top right corner.
2. Set up your account.
3. Link your bank account under **Payroll and Tax > Direct Deposit** so you can get paid.

Send "done" for ADM-03 with the [reflection form](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task). **Never put bank or account details in the form.**
