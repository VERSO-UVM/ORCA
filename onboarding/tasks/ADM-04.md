## Why it matters

You are paid for the hours you enter. Missed entries mean late pay.

## Steps

1. Enter your time in PeopleSoft under **UVM Time Entry > Enter Time** after every shift, or at least once a week.
2. Set a weekly reminder on your phone for the end of the week, so you never miss one.
3. If you lead a Pod, remind your team to do the same each week.

Returning students: just confirm your time entry still works.

## How to finish

Once your reminder is set, send "done" for ADM-04 with the [reflection form](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task).
