## Why it matters

These six activities, spread over the project lifecycle, help you settle into leading a team.

## Steps

1. **Meet your team.** Learn their strengths and weaknesses, what motivates them, and what they need to succeed.
2. **Set goals with your team.** Make them clear, measurable and achievable.
3. **Plan how to reach them,** with a timeline and a list of tasks.
4. **Delegate** based on strengths, with clear instructions and deadlines.
5. **Watch progress and give feedback,** so everyone stays on track.
6. **Celebrate your team's successes.** It keeps people motivated and engaged.
