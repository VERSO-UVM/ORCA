## Why it matters

The [Open Resource Library](https://www.openresourcelibrary.com/) is ORCA's community resource for understanding open source software and open practices. As a community project, it needs your input, and improving it is a low-stakes way to make your first pull request.

## Steps

1. Review the [Tools](https://www.openresourcelibrary.com/category/tools) section, and make sure you know the tools that matter for your role.
2. Review the rest of the site.
3. In the [repository](https://github.com/VERSO-UVM/Open-Resource-Library), fix a typo, add context, or update the content of one page.
4. Open a pull request with your change.

## How to finish

Post your reflection on your tracking issue (see [How to finish a task](../README.md#how-to-finish-a-task)) with a link to your pull request. Tick the box once the pull request is accepted.
