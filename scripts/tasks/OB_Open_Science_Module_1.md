Transform to Open Science (TOPS) was part of NASA’s [Open-Source Science Initiative](https://science.nasa.gov/researchers/open-science/) which is making a long-term commitment to create a scientific culture that is ready for 21st century challenges. In prior years we used the Open Science 101 course as onboarding material but in 2025 this was removed from the public.

This is where open source matters; the course was originally released under an open Creative Commons license, and we had forked the repo when it existed. So we gave it new life by taking the original materials, extending them and repurposing for our needs.

The curriculum is available on [Open Science 101](https://verso-uvm.github.io/Open-Science-101/)

1. Go to: [Open Science 101](https://verso-uvm.github.io/Open-Science-101/)
2. Review Module 1
3. Complete the 5 activities in Module 1, add them as comments (you can just do it with markdown in the comment's field) to the issue and @kefortney with your ORCID when done.
4. Close the issue.
